The problem

You pay for sales help. You still do not get enough sales calls.

Here are four ways companies try to get more sales calls. Each one has the same problem.

Referrals

You wait for referrals.

Some months you get a lot. Some months you get none. You cannot control it.

Marketing agency

You pay an agency every month.

You pay the same bill if you get ten sales calls or zero.

Reports

You get reports, not calls.

The report shows how many people opened an email. It does not show buyers you talked to.

A new hire

You hire someone to book calls.

You pay them each month and buy their tools. It takes months to know if it worked.

None of them promised you a set number of sales calls. We do.

How it works

Here is what we do, in three steps.

We do the work. You take the calls.

  1. Step 01

    We count your buyers.

    We make a list of the companies you sell to. We find the owners and founders there. We check that each work email is real.

    Then we work out how many of them we need to email to book one sales call.

    If there are enough buyers, we start. If there are not, we tell you.

    How we count

    #Buyers we can reach
    ÷Buyers we email to book one call
    =Sales calls we can book for you

  2. Step 02

    We email your buyers.

    We set up the email accounts. We build the list of buyers. We write the emails. Then we send them.

    You read the emails and say yes before we send any. You also agree on which buyers count as a qualified call.

    What we set up for you

    • New email accounts to send from. Your main email is not used.
    • A list of buyers with checked email addresses
    • The emails, written by us and approved by you
    • We answer each reply and book the call

  3. Step 03

    You take the calls.

    We answer every reply. We find out who is a real buyer. We put the call on your calendar.

    Before each call, we tell you who the person is and why they said yes.

    Your job is to show up and sell.

    What you get with each call

    • The buyer's name, job title and company
    • What they wrote back to us
    • A set time on your calendar

What is a qualified call?

It is a sales call that happens with a buyer you agreed to. Before we send any email, we write down who counts. That means the kind of company, its size and the person's job. You approve it.

How does "or you don't pay" work?

It means what it says. We explain each detail on the call, after we count your buyers.

How much does it cost?

We tell you the price on the call. First we need to know if we can reach enough buyers for you.

What if there are not enough buyers?

Then we tell you, and we do not start. We only work with companies we can get calls for.

When do I get my first call?

New email accounts cannot send a lot of email on day one. So the first weeks are setup, and the calls start after that. We go over the timing on the call.

What do you need from me?

One call to tell us what you sell and who your best customers are. Your yes on the emails. Your yes on who counts as a qualified call. Then someone to take the calls each week.

Do the emails come from my email address?

No. We send from new email accounts that we set up. Your main email is not used. The emails still have your company's name on them, and you approve them first.
