How it works

We do the math on your market before we promise anything.

A number like 40 only means something if your market can deliver it. So the arithmetic comes first, and you see it.

  1. Step 01

    Count the market.

    We pull the companies that fit your customer profile and the owners and founders at them whose work email we can find and verify. Then we work out how many of those people we need to email to book one qualified call.

    If the count supports 40 calls with room to spare, we go ahead. If it does not, we tell you.

    #Buyers we can reach in your market
    ÷Buyers we email to book one qualified call
    =Calls your market can support

  2. Step 02

    Send at volume.

    Booking 40 calls in 90 days takes a lot of email, sent well. We build the sending setup, the lists and the copy, then email your market at the volume the count calls for.

    You approve the copy and the definition of a qualified call before the first email goes out.

    What we run for you

    • Sending domains and inboxes, separate from your main domain
    • Lead lists built for your market, with verified emails
    • Email copy, written by us and approved by you
    • Reply handling and booking, through to the calendar invite
    • A running count of qualified calls against the 40

  3. Step 03

    Take the calls.

    We answer the replies, sort out who is worth your time and put the qualified calls on your calendar. You know who you are talking to and why they said yes before you join.

    Your job is to show up and sell. 40 calls in 90 days is about 3 a week.

    What comes with each call

    • Their name, title and company
    • The reply that led to the booking
    • A time on your calendar

Who it is for

We only take on markets where the numbers work.

Every company goes through the same fit check before we take it on. Here is what we look for.

A fit

  • B2B company with 20 or more employeesYou sell to other businesses and have a team behind the offer.
  • You sell to owners and foundersThe person who says yes to you owns or runs the company.
  • Your offer brings in customers or revenueYou can put a number on the result, and you have proof to show for it.
  • A large marketThere are enough companies to reach 40 calls. We count this for you.
  • We handle the replies and the bookingWe answer every reply and book the calls ourselves, so nothing sits waiting in an inbox.

Not a fit

  • You sell to consumersCold email to work inboxes is a B2B channel.
  • Your buyer is not the owner or founderIf the decision sits with a department or a buying committee, this is not built for it.
  • The result is hard to put a number onWe need a customer or revenue outcome, and proof behind it.
  • Your whole market is a few hundred companiesThe count will not support 40 calls, and we will say so.
  • Nobody is free to take the callsSomeone on your side has to show up to about 3 calls a week.

Questions

What people ask before they book.

Anything else, ask us on the call.

What counts as a qualified call?

We write the definition down with you before anything is sent: who the buyer is, the company size and the kind of company you want to talk to. A call counts when it is held with someone who fits that definition.

How does the guarantee work?

The short version is the headline: 40 qualified sales calls in 90 days, or we refund every call we miss. We go through the details on the call, once we have looked at your market.

What does it cost?

We cover pricing on the call, after we know whether your market supports the number. There is no point quoting a price for something we cannot deliver.

What if my market is too small?

Then we tell you and that is the end of it. We only take on companies that pass the fit check, and market size is part of it.

When does the first call show up?

New sending domains need a warm up period before they can carry volume, so the first weeks are setup and the calls build from there. We walk you through the timeline on the call.

What do you need from me?

A kickoff call to walk us through your offer and your best customers. Your sign off on the copy and on the definition of a qualified call. Then someone to take about 3 calls a week.

Do the emails come from my domain?

No. We send from separate domains set up for the campaign, so your main domain stays out of it. The emails are still in your company's name and you approve them first.
