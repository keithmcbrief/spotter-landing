Who it is for

We only take on markets where the numbers work.

Every company goes through the same fit check before we take it on. Here is what we look for.

A fit

  • B2B company with 20 or more employeesYou sell to other businesses and have a team behind the offer.
  • You sell to owners and foundersThe person who says yes to you owns or runs the company.
  • Your offer brings in customers or revenueYou can put a number on the result, and you have proof to show for it.
  • A large marketThere are enough companies to reach the number. We count this for you.
  • We handle the replies and the bookingWe answer every reply and book the calls ourselves, so nothing sits waiting in an inbox.

Not a fit

  • You sell to consumersCold email to work inboxes is a B2B channel.
  • Your buyer is not the owner or founderIf the decision sits with a department or a buying committee, this is not built for it.
  • The result is hard to put a number onWe need a customer or revenue outcome, and proof behind it.
  • Your whole market is a few hundred companiesThe count will not support the number, and we will say so.
  • Nobody is free to take the callsSomeone on your side has to show up to several calls a week.

Back to the calendar
What counts as a qualified call?

We write the definition down with you before anything is sent: who the buyer is, the company size and the kind of company you want to talk to. A call counts when it is held with someone who fits that definition.

How does the guarantee work?

The short version is the headline at the top of this page. We go through the details on the call, once we have looked at your market.

What does it cost?

We cover pricing on the call, after we know whether your market supports the number. There is no point quoting a price for something we cannot deliver.

What if my market is too small?

Then we tell you and that is the end of it. We only take on companies that pass the fit check, and market size is part of it.

When does the first call show up?

New sending domains need a warm up period before they can carry volume, so the first weeks are setup and the calls build from there. We walk you through the timeline on the call.

What do you need from me?

A kickoff call to walk us through your offer and your best customers. Your sign off on the copy and on the definition of a qualified call. Then someone to take the calls each week.

Do the emails come from my domain?

No. We send from separate domains set up for the campaign, so your main domain stays out of it. The emails are still in your company's name and you approve them first.
