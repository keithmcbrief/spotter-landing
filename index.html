How it works

We do the math on your market before you pay.

A guarantee is only worth something if the market can deliver it. So the arithmetic comes first, and you see it.

  1. Step 01

    Count the market.

    We pull the companies that fit your customer profile and the decision makers at them whose work email we can find and verify. Then we work out how many of those people we need to email to book one qualified meeting. That tells us how many months of 40 meetings your market holds.

    If the count supports 40 meetings a month with room to spare, we offer the guarantee. If it does not, we tell you, and you keep your money.

    ÷Buyers we email to book one qualified meeting
    =Meetings your market can support

  2. Step 02

    Send at volume.

    Booking 40 meetings a month takes a lot of email, sent well. We build the sending setup, the lists and the copy, then email your market at the volume the count calls for. We answer the replies and put the qualified meetings on your calendar.

    You approve the copy and the definition of a qualified meeting. After that, your job is to take the meetings.

    What we run for you

    • Sending domains and inboxes, separate from your main domain
    • Lead lists built for your market, with verified emails
    • Email copy, written by us and approved by you
    • Reply handling and booking, through to the calendar invite
    • A running count of qualified meetings against the 40

  3. Step 03

    Refund the gap.

    At the end of each month we count the meetings that were held with a qualified buyer. If the number is 40 or more, we did our job. If it is less, we refund the difference, meeting by meeting.

    A no-show counts as a miss. So does a cancellation, and so does a meeting with someone who does not fit the definition we agreed on. You only pay for meetings that were real.

Who it is for

We only guarantee markets where the numbers work.

That rules some companies out. Better you hear it here than after you have paid someone.

A fit

  • B2B company with 20 or more employeesYou sell to other businesses and have a team behind the offer.
  • You sell nationallyYour buyers are spread across the country, not one metro area.
  • Enough reachable buyersThere are enough companies in your market to support 40 meetings a month, for more than one month. We check this for you.
  • Buyers with findable job titlesThe people who say yes show up in contact databases under a title we can search for.
  • Deals big enough to carry the feeA few closed deals a month should cover the fee. PLACEHOLDER: minimum deal size

Not a fit

  • You sell to consumersCold email to work inboxes is a B2B channel.
  • You only serve one city or regionLocal markets run out of buyers too fast to support 40 meetings a month.
  • Your whole market is a few hundred companiesThe count will not support the guarantee, and we will say so.
  • Your buyers are hard to find by titleIf they are not in contact databases, we cannot reach enough of them.
  • Nobody is free to take the meetings40 a month is about 10 a week. Someone on your side has to show up to them.

Questions

What people ask before they book.

Anything else, ask us on the call.

What counts as a qualified meeting?

We write the definition down with you before anything is sent: the job titles, the company size and the kind of company you want to talk to. A meeting counts when it is held with someone who fits that definition. Anything else does not count toward the 40.

What happens when someone does not show?

That meeting is refunded. No-shows and cancellations are treated the same as a meeting we never booked, so you are not paying for an empty slot on your calendar. PLACEHOLDER: how rescheduled meetings are treated

How does the refund work?

Each of the 40 meetings is an equal share of the monthly fee. At the end of the month we count the qualified meetings that were held, and every one short of 40 is refunded at that share. Hold 33, and 7 shares come back to you. PLACEHOLDER: when and how the refund is paid

What does it cost?

One fee per month, paid up front. PLACEHOLDER: monthly price, or "quoted after we count your market"

Why do I pay up front?

Sending enough email to book 40 meetings a month costs real money from the first day: domains, inboxes, data and the people running it. The fee pays for that. The refund is what keeps us honest about the number we promised.

What if my market is too small?

Then we tell you on the first call and we do not take your money. We would rather turn a market down than sell a guarantee the numbers cannot back.

When does the first meeting show up?

New sending domains need a warm up period before they can carry volume, so the first weeks are setup. We walk you through the timeline before you pay. PLACEHOLDER: ramp timeline, and when the first guaranteed month starts

What do you need from me?

A kickoff call to walk us through your offer and your best customers. Your sign off on the copy and on the definition of a qualified meeting. Then someone to take about 10 meetings a week.

Do the emails come from my domain?

No. We send from separate domains set up for the campaign, so your main domain stays out of it. The emails are still in your company's name and you approve them first.
