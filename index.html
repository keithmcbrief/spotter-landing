How it works

We do the math on your market before you pay.

A guarantee is only worth something if the market can deliver it. So the arithmetic comes first, and you see it.

  1. Step 01

    Count the market.

    We pull the companies that fit your customer profile and the decision makers at them whose work email we can find and verify. Then we work out how many of those people we need to email to book one qualified call.

    If the count clears 100 calls with room to spare, we offer the guarantee. If it does not, we tell you, and you keep your money.

    The count

    #Buyers we can reach in your market
    ÷Buyers we email to book one qualified call
    =Calls your market can support

  2. Step 02

    Send at volume.

    Hitting 100 calls in 90 days takes a lot of email, sent well. We build the sending setup, the lists and the copy, then email your market at the volume the count calls for. We answer the replies and put the qualified calls on your calendar.

    You approve the copy and the definition of a qualified call. After that, your job is to take the calls.

    What we run for you

    • Sending domains and inboxes, separate from your main domain
    • Lead lists built for your market, with verified emails
    • Email copy, written by us and approved by you
    • Reply handling and booking, through to the calendar invite
    • A running count of qualified calls against the 100

  3. Step 03

    Refund the gap.

    At day 90 we count the qualified calls together. If the number is 100 or more, we did our job. If it is less, we refund each missing call at one hundredth of the fee.

    Deliver 82, refund 18. There is nothing to negotiate, because the definition of a qualified call was agreed before the first email went out.

Who it is for

We only guarantee markets where the numbers work.

That rules some companies out. Better you hear it here than after you have paid someone.

A fit

  • B2B company with 20 or more employeesYou sell to other businesses and have a team behind the offer.
  • You sell nationallyYour buyers are spread across the country, not one metro area.
  • Enough reachable buyersThere are enough companies in your market to support 100 calls. We check this for you.
  • Buyers with findable job titlesThe people who say yes show up in contact databases under a title we can search for.
  • Deals big enough to carry the feeA handful of closed deals from 100 calls should pay for the whole thing. PLACEHOLDER: minimum deal size

Not a fit

  • You sell to consumersCold email to work inboxes is a B2B channel.
  • You only serve one city or regionLocal markets run out of buyers before we reach 100 calls.
  • Your whole market is a few hundred companiesThe count will not support the guarantee, and we will say so.
  • Your buyers are hard to find by titleIf they are not in contact databases, we cannot reach enough of them.
  • Nobody is free to take the calls100 calls in 90 days is about 8 a week. Someone on your side has to show up to them.

Questions

What people ask before they book.

Anything else, ask us on the call.

What counts as a qualified call?

We write the definition down with you before anything is sent: the job titles, the company size and the kind of company you want to talk to. A call counts when it is with someone who fits that definition. Calls that do not fit do not count toward the 100. PLACEHOLDER: counts when held or when booked

How does the refund work?

Divide the fee by 100. That is the price of one call. At day 90 we count the qualified calls, and every call short of 100 is refunded at that price. If we deliver 82, you get 18% of the fee back. PLACEHOLDER: when the refund is paid

What does it cost?

One fee, paid up front, covering the full 90 days. PLACEHOLDER: price, or "quoted after we count your market"

Why do I pay up front?

Sending enough email to book 100 calls costs real money from the first day: domains, inboxes, data and the people running it. The fee pays for that. The refund is what keeps us honest about the number we promised.

What if my market is too small?

Then we tell you on the first call and we do not take your money. We would rather turn a market down than sell a guarantee the numbers cannot back.

When does the first call show up?

New sending domains need a warm up period before they can carry volume, so the first weeks are setup and the calls build from there. We walk you through the timeline before you pay. PLACEHOLDER: ramp timeline, and when the 90 days start

What do you need from me?

A kickoff call to walk us through your offer and your best customers. Your sign off on the copy and on the definition of a qualified call. Then someone to take about 8 calls a week.

Do the emails come from my domain?

No. We send from separate domains set up for the campaign, so your main domain stays out of it. The emails are still in your company's name and you approve them first.
