Questions people ask before they book.

If yours is not here, ask us on the call.

What is a qualified call?

A sales call that actually happens with a buyer who matches the criteria we agree on in writing before launch: company type, company size, and decision maker title. No-shows and off-fit contacts do not count.

How does "or you don't pay" work?

If we finish below your guaranteed call number, you get a proportional refund for every call we miss, written directly into the agreement. We walk through the exact terms on the call after counting your market.

How much does it cost?

We share pricing on the call once we confirm your market size and call target. Everything is included, with no surprise add-on fees for domains, inboxes, or data.

What if there are not enough buyers?

We tell you on the first call and do not take the engagement. We only guarantee call counts when the market math supports them.

When do I get my first call?

First calls typically land within 2 to 3 weeks of kickoff while we build your verified buyer list, dedicated sending domains, and approved scripts.

What do you need from me?

One kickoff call on your offer and best customers, your sign-off on the outbound copy and qualification criteria, and someone ready to take qualified sales calls each week.

Do the emails come from my email address?

No. We buy and configure dedicated lookalike domains and inboxes so your primary domain is never touched. Every email still represents your company and goes out only after you approve it.
